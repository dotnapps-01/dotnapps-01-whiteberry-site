Questions

Good to know

Can’t find your answer? Message us — we reply personally.

How do I place an order?

Choose a piece, pick your size (or “Made to measure”), add it to your bag and check out. We then confirm your measurements, payment and delivery date with you on WhatsApp.

How can I pay?

At checkout you can confirm on WhatsApp and pay by UPI or a payment link, pay online, or choose cash on delivery — whichever options are shown at checkout are available for your order.

Can pieces be stitched to my measurements?

That’s what Whiteberry is about — blouses, skirts and anarkalis are cut to your measure. Share your measurements when you enquire, or ask us how to take them.

Can I change the colour or the dupatta?

Often, yes. Tell us what you have in mind — a different dupatta, a contrast blouse, another colour of the same print — and we’ll confirm what’s possible.

How long will it take?

It depends on the piece and your date. Share the occasion date when you enquire and we’ll confirm a realistic timeline before you commit.

Do you deliver outside the city?

Ask us on WhatsApp — we’ll confirm delivery options for your location.
